I am a postdoctoral scholar at the University of Central Florida, working with Dr. Helen J. Huang and Dr. Yue Wen. My research bridges biological and machine intelligence to create wearable robotic systems for rehabilitation, human augmentation, and mobility assistance. I work across three interconnected areas: developing generalizable neural decoding methods that translate signals such as HD-sEMG and EEG into estimates of motor intent; studying human motor control through neuromusculoskeletal modeling and deep reinforcement learning; and designing biomechanically informed wearable robots with human-centered control strategies. Together, these efforts form a Human Movement Intelligence framework that unites neural decoding, computational motor control, and wearable robotics to advance adaptive, personalized, and deployable assistive technologies.
